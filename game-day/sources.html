Sports Brain: trivia

How many players does each soccer team have on the field?
11. That includes the goalkeeper. Same number as an American football team!		IFAB: Laws of the Game
How long is a professional soccer match, not counting added time?
90 minutes. Two halves of 45 minutes. The referee adds extra minutes at the end of each half for time lost to injuries and subs.		IFAB: Laws of the Game
Which player is allowed to use their hands inside their own penalty area?
The goalkeeper. Outside the penalty area, even the goalkeeper can't use their hands.		IFAB: Laws of the Game
What color card does a referee show to send a player off?
Red. Two yellow cards in the same game add up to a red, and the team plays with one fewer player.		IFAB: Laws of the Game
How far is the penalty spot from the goal line?
12 yards. That's 11 meters. The goalkeeper has to keep at least part of one foot on the line until the ball is kicked.		IFAB: Laws of the Game
The ball goes over the goal line, but not in the goal, and an attacker touched it last. What's the restart?
A goal kick. If a defender touched it last, the attacking team gets a corner kick instead.		IFAB: Laws of the Game
A defender touches the ball last before it goes over their own goal line (not in the goal). What does the other team get?
A corner kick. Corners are a great chance to score.		IFAB: Laws of the Game
How does a team restart play when the ball goes out over the sideline?
A throw-in. The thrower has to use both hands, bring the ball from behind the head, and keep both feet on the ground.		IFAB: Laws of the Game
What is it called when a player scores three goals in one game?
A hat-trick. The phrase started in cricket in the 1800s, when a bowler who took three wickets in a row was given a hat.		Hat-trick
When a goalkeeper doesn't let in a single goal all game, it's called a...?
Clean sheet. In the USA it's also called a shutout.		Glossary of association football terms
What is a "nutmeg"?
Kicking the ball between a defender's legs. Players love doing it and hate having it done to them.		Glossary of association football terms
What is "offside"?
Being closer to the goal than the ball and the second-to-last defender when a teammate passes to you. The goalkeeper usually counts as one of those last two defenders. You can't be offside in your own half, or straight from a throw-in, goal kick or corner kick.		IFAB: Laws of the Game
At kickoff, where is the ball placed?
On the center spot. Every player has to be in their own half until the ball is kicked.		IFAB: Laws of the Game
How many substitutes can a team usually use in a top professional game today?
5. For many years it was only 3. Five subs were first allowed in 2020 and made permanent in 2022.		IFAB: five substitutes made permanent
What does "VAR" stand for?
Video Assistant Referee. VAR was first used at a men's World Cup in 2018 in Russia.		FIFA: VAR at the 2018 World Cup
If a knockout World Cup game is tied after extra time, what happens?
A penalty shootout. Each team takes five penalties. If it's still tied, they keep going one kick each until one team scores and the other misses.		IFAB: Laws of the Game
What does the assistant referee (the linesman) carry?
A flag. They raise the flag for offside, throw-ins and fouls the referee might miss.		IFAB: Laws of the Game
What is it called when a player accidentally scores in their own net?
An own goal. It still counts, but for the other team.		Glossary of association football terms
Which body part are field players NOT allowed to play the ball with?
Their hands and arms. A handball gives the other team a free kick, or a penalty if it happens in the box.		IFAB: Laws of the Game
Traditionally, which shirt number does a team's starting goalkeeper wear?
1. Goalkeepers also wear a different color from everyone else, so the referee can tell who's allowed to use their hands.		Squad number (association football)
Pelé, Maradona and Messi all made which shirt number famous?
10. The number 10 usually goes to a team's most creative attacking player.		Squad number (association football)
